# AI Search / GEO Rules

For SEO and AI-answer visibility:
- Prefer answer-first, self-contained sections.
- Make important answers understandable without surrounding context.
- Use real questions customers ask.
- Include useful FAQs where relevant.
- Build topical authority through related content and internal links.
- Use specific, supportable facts and sources where useful.
- Keep entity names, product names, URLs, and business facts accurate.
- Do not fabricate citations, reviews, statistics, expert opinions, or partnerships.
