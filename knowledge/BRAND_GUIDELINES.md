# Aslam Beauty Brand Guidelines

## Visual identity
- Primary purple: #7B5EA7
- Deep purple: #5B3F8C
- Light purple: #9B7EC8
- Lavender: #C4A8E0
- Dark navy/text: #1A1A2E
- Gold accent: #F5A623
- Backgrounds: white and near-white tones
- Headings: Fraunces Serif
- Body/UI: Inter

## Brand character
- Minimal
- Premium
- Clean
- Spa-inspired
- Sophisticated but approachable

## Design principles
- Use generous whitespace.
- Use deep navy and the purple family as the primary visual system.
- Keep typography editorial and premium.
- Do not introduce orange-heavy, beige-heavy, or unrelated brand systems.
- Product packaging must remain visually accurate. Do not add an Aslam Beauty logo to third-party product packaging.
