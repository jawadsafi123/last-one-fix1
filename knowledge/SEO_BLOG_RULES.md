# Aslam Beauty SEO and Blog Rules

Use the connected Shopify store as the source of truth for products, existing blogs, categories, pricing, URLs, media, and store content when available.

## Research
- Understand search intent before writing.
- Research current search results and People Also Ask questions.
- Use keyword research when available.
- Check competitors and content gaps.
- Consider Google SEO plus answer-engine/AI-search visibility.

## Writing
- Answer the core question early.
- Use clear question-style headings where appropriate.
- Use short paragraphs, lists, tables, and FAQs.
- Use natural keywords, never keyword stuffing.
- Link relevant products and related content.
- Do not invent claims, reviews, studies, awards, testimonials, prices, or product facts.
- Avoid em dashes.

## Approval gates
Gate 1: confirm title/style, products, and media plan before writing when the user has not already specified them.
Gate 2: show the complete draft for review before publishing.

## Publishing
Default to Shopify draft content. Never publish without explicit approval.
